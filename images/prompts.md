# Iron Protocol — Art Generation Guide

This guide compiles the prompts and parameters required to generate consistent, black-and-white, pen-and-ink technical illustrations for *Iron Protocol*, matching the classic **BattleTech Technical Readout (TRO)** manual aesthetic.

---

## 🎨 The Core Style Formula
When generating any image for *Iron Protocol*, always append or prefix the following style parameters to ensure visual cohesion:

* **Style Keywords**: `Technical line art, wargame concept art sketch, black and white pen and ink, cross-hatching shadows, clean white background, gritty industrial design, no color.`
* **Anti-Text Protection**: To prevent the generator from creating gibberish text or labels in the drawings, explicitly include: `Completely clean art with no text, no labels, no words, and no titles.`
* **Aspect Ratio**: Recommended aspect ratio is `--ar 3:4` or `--ar 1:1` for layout boxes.
